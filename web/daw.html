Latency calibration

Recording arrives late by however long your speakers → air → microphone → driver round trip takes. This wizard measures that once per input device and the recorder then places every take earlier by exactly that offset, so what you played on the beat sits on the beat.

It needs your speakers audible to your microphone: turn the volume to a normal level, keep the room quiet, and don't use headphones — the mic must hear the chirp.

Track colour
