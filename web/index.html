    Cover a song

    The song you give becomes a timbre and style reference (experimental: ComfyUI's "Set Reference Audio"), rendered in your style and lyrics; how closely it keeps the melody has not been measured here. The planner is off for a cover. A LoRA carries its own licence; some forbid commercial use.

    Melody & score Hum a melody, or cover a song
    8s

    Hum: seconds. Whole song: a minute or more, and longer the first time its voice is separated.

    How many seconds of the original performance YuE2 hears before it starts — it hears the Studio's reading of them, not the recording, and none of the original audio is in the result. Longer is not better: past a few seconds it re-performs the original's own arrangement instead of yours. 0 uses the score alone. Starting from one layer — its drums for the groove, its voice for the phrasing — sends that layer instead of the mix.

          What an agent cannot do
          
    
            
    
              
    Export a video file
    
              
    Export is a real-time capture in the browser, so there is no server-side renderer to call.
                build_music_video writes a project and a person presses Export.
    
              
    Restyle more than five seconds in one call
    
              
    One LTX run is 121 frames. Longer videos are several runs chained, each seeded from the
                last frame of the one before.
    
              
    Render two things at once
    
              
    Covers, images, clips and restyles share one queue and music preempts it — one
                graphics card. A cloud model can still talk while a render runs.
    
            
    
          
    
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    Reactive
    
      
    Build a video around your music. Choose the song region and source media, set the look, then review a short test or the full render. Cuts and effects use the compositor; Paint and Motion also run a diffusion model.
    
      

      
    
        
    
        
    1 · Music and source
    
        
        
        
    
          
          
        
    
        
    Blank length uses the remaining song, up to 10 minutes; Motion is capped at 120 seconds. The source clip's timing is separate.
    
        
    

        
    
          Pictures none picked · click to pick, in the order they should appear
          
    
          
    Any pictures from the Pictures library. Two or more read as a
            sequence; one reads as a breathing still.
    
        
    

        
    
          Clips · optional: a clip plays in sync with the song in its slot
          
    
          
    Cuts use your selected media in order. Paint and Motion repaint the first selected clip, with the selected pictures as visual references. Extra clips are ignored by those two looks.
    
        
    
        
    Your selection, in order
    Pick pictures or clips above.

    2 · Choose the look
    
        
    
          Look
          
    
          
    
          
    
            Paint settings · one diffusion render per frame
            
    How hard each frame is repainted 0.2–0.95; the figure is held by the source, so this can be high
              
    
            
    …and how much more on a loud bass
              
    
            
    How hard the source re-asserts itself each frame 0 = free-running Deforum, 1 = no feedback
              
    
            
    Colour held to the first painted frame
              
    
            
    Frames per second each frame is one image render
              
    
            
    Seed
    
            
    Pick the clip to repaint in the Clips grid; the pictures you pick are the look and take turns
              on the bars. NVIDIA only. Try a short region first; render time depends on your hardware and settings.
    
          
    
          
    
            Motion recipe and source timing
            
            
    Standard keeps the existing AnimateDiff v3 recipe. The experimental LCM remix keeps structure controls and beat-scheduled image conditioning, with anchors and the detail pass off by default. Optional reference anchoring can overwhelm the source.
    
            
            
            
    Look prompts one per line, used when no pictures are selected; blank uses the built-in palettes
              
    
            
    Structure, anchors, sampling and detail settings
            
            
            
    Depth hold 0–1.5 · left alone: 0.4 with pictures (keeps the figure), 0.2 with prompts (paints over the room)
              
    
            
    Line hold 0–1.5 · left alone: 0.5 with pictures, 0.25 with prompts
              
    
            
    Iris on the bass 0–1 · a black card with a round hole in it over the whole piece; at rest the circle sits inside the frame, on every bass hit it opens past the edges. 0 is off
              
    
            
    How hard it moves 0.1–3 · AnimateDiff's own motion scale; 1 is the module's own, and every earlier piece was rendered at 1. Above about 1.5 motion becomes churn
              
    
            
    
              
    
            
    Anchor strength 0–2 · default 0. With reference pictures selected, 1 tries the published workflow's anchoring; matching its appearance remains unverified
              
    
            
    Anchor holds until 0.1–1 · how far through the render the hit's picture stays anchored
              
    
            
    Depth hold until 0.1–1 · how far through the render the figure's volumes stay held; left alone 0.6 with pictures, 0.5 with prompts
              
    
            
    Line hold until 0.1–1 · the same for her edges
              
    
            
    Prompt strength (cfg) left alone: 7 with pictures, 8 with prompts
              
    
            
    Picture weight 0–2 · how hard the pictures you picked set the look
              
    
            
    Switch length frames of cross-fade ending on each hit
              
    
            
    Switch on Standard: beats or bars. LCM remix: peaks measured from the drum audio, independent of the beat grid
              
    
            
    Least gap between hits frames · 5 is the reference's; 11 = every other beat at 128 bpm
              
    
            
    Your own motion module files in the engine's animatediff_models folder — nothing shipped; blank = the v3 module
              
    
            
    Your own motion LoRA animatediff_motion_lora folder; strength beside it
              
              
    
            
    Your own model LoRA loras folder; strength beside it. LCM remix leaves the v3 domain adapter out
              
              
    
            
    Sampler · schedule blank = dpmpp_2m · karras; lcm · sgm_uniform with an LCM module and cfg 2 is the reference's
              
              
    
            
    Detail pass Standard uses latent 2×; LCM remix uses pixel 1.5× when enabled. LCM remix defaults off for one pass
              
    
            
    Detail repaint 0.2–0.9 · how much the second pass repaints
              
    
            
    Smooth to 24 fps motion-interpolated from the 12 fps render
              
    
            
    Seed
    
            
    Pick a clip to repaint and pictures for its look. Image conditioning changes on the drum
              hits; without pictures, the prompts above change on the bars. Temporal motion, depth and line art
              guide the result. Speed and appearance depend on the chosen profile. Requires NVIDIA and
              ComfyUI-AnimateDiff-Evolved in the local engine.

    What you need
    
        
    
          
    To generate
    
            
    A modern graphics card. There is no strict minimum: smaller cards stream
              the model and mostly mean more waiting. A three-minute song takes four to
              five minutes on a strong card.
    
          
    To edit
    
            
    No big card needed. The image editor and the VFX compositor run on the
              CPU. Only generation waits for the graphics card.

      Why the small AI marker has no switch
          
      Exports always carry a small machine-readable marker on AI-generated parts. The model
            licences and, since 2 August 2026, the EU AI Act (Article 50) require the tool to
            do this, so you never have to. The switch above controls the detailed record:
            prompts, seeds, lyrics and edit history. That record is yours. Hiding badges only
            changes what is drawn on screen.
