1 · Welcome and setup

Welcome to our AIPLAY study group! Pick the official release for your platform, then make one feature ready.

Windows Setup 1.5: https://github.com/Senzube4n/AIPLAY-Studio/releases/tag/setup-v1.5
Linux desktop and Mac preview have separate chapters in our updated guide. Models/engines remain separate. The automatic native YuE2 GGUF runtime is Windows x64 only.

If an old Setup omitted shared/worker, close Studio and install the newest Setup into the same folder. If antivirus quarantines Setup, keep it quarantined and use the documented source alternative; do not disable protection. Windows battery monitoring is explicit opt-in.

Share your build line/platform and which feature is ready. Crop keys/private paths. What would you like to make first?

2 · Linux desktop setup

Linux session: choose the Intel/AMD 64-bit or ARM64 package matching your computer.

https://github.com/Senzube4n/AIPLAY-Studio/releases/tag/linux-setup-v1.0

Open the .deb in your software installer, Install, then launch AIPLAY Studio from the application menu. First setup needs GitHub/npm access and supplies Node/npm for the app. Models are separate.

Targets: Ubuntu 22.04/24.04 x64 and Ubuntu 24.04 ARM64. The automatic native YuE2 GGUF runtime remains Windows x64 only. Compatible music/image/video engines need their own setup. Do not run Studio with sudo.

Share your build line, distro and app/engine readiness without private paths or keys.

3 · Mac developer preview

Mac session: choose Apple silicon ARM64 or Intel x64 from the preview release.

https://github.com/Senzube4n/AIPLAY-Studio/releases/tag/macos-setup-v1.0

Open the DMG, drag AIPLAY Studio to Applications, eject the DMG and open the copied app. First setup supplies private Node/npm and needs GitHub/npm access.

Declared minimum macOS 13.5; app acceptance covered 14/26 Apple silicon and 15 Intel. Ad-hoc signed, no Developer ID or notarization; Gatekeeper may block it. Keep protections enabled and report the exact message. App/DAW startup is not proof that local YuE2, H3 or MOSS generation works.

Models and compatible engines are separate. Tell us which step you reached, without private data.

4 · A first YuE2 song

This week: one musical idea, a few takes, one clear observation.

Music → Simple is the quick start. Song or Advanced gives direct Style/Lyrics controls; the label/layout varies by build. This automatic native GGUF runtime is Windows x64 only. Native GGUF needs nonempty style and lyrics; put [Verse] and [Chorus] on their own lines. Keep the recipe and listen through the ending.

Try two or three seeds with the same words. Share a short excerpt you may share, the engine/model and the one result you noticed. A new seed is a new performance, not a guaranteed improvement.

Discussion: which take tells the song best, and where does it lose you?

5 · Pictures and references

Let us build one visual world before making lots of clips.

In Full Studio, open Pictures. Choose a ready engine, describe one subject + framing + environment + light, then Make image. If that engine supports Reference images, try your subject in a second setting.

Keep the useful take and compare face, silhouette, wardrobe and background influence. Use the editor for titles and ordinary cleanup. Read each model’s terms.

Share two public-safe images and a focused question: what stayed consistent, and what drifted?

6 · MOSS recipe comparison

MOSS session: choose one neutral prompt, seed and canvas before comparing.

Models → MOSS-Image SFT. Pictures → Advanced → MOSS-Image (experimental, text only) → Standard, 1024 square, count 1. Click check, get files if needed, then Make image once ready. Keep the result/recipe.

Try an explicit preset: Spectrum H1/H2 (separate source pack and idle restart), Turbo 4, DMD 8, Kromoss Standard/Kromoss DMD2, or MOSS HiRes (4 MP). Each has its own fixed recipe. HiRes makes a NEW 2048-square picture, not an upscale.

Qwen remains automatic/reference/editing; MOSS has no reference/edit path here. Share actual results, timing and detail changes, not a universal speed/quality claim.

https://github.com/Senzube4n/AIPLAY-Studio/blob/main/docs/MOSS_IMAGE.md

7 · Optional adapter, neutral exercise

Optional MOSS recipe, with no explicit imagery needed: install MOSS NSFW Uncensored (optional), then choose NSFW Uncensored in Pictures.

Its model-only adapter uses strength 0.7, 30 steps, CFG 3, ER-SDE/simple. Begin the prompt with moss_xvstyle; Studio does not insert it. Use a fully clothed adult portrait to learn the controls. This stays an explicit choice, not a global default.

The publisher supplies Apache-2.0 for this adapter. Example content/quality has not been validated; reference/output rights remain separate.

Share only a neutral public-safe screenshot of the recipe and what you actually tested.
https://github.com/Senzube4n/AIPLAY-Studio/blob/main/docs/MOSS_IMAGE.md#optional-nsfw-uncensored-adapter

8 · YuE2 training, honestly

Adapter study session: write the listening question before pressing Train.

Two separate paths: Training uses one reviewed recording region for an experimental ComfyUI Audio LoRA; Music Lab → Native tools trains from a reviewed dataset with a separate native pack. The native dataset trainer currently requires NVIDIA CUDA and at least 11 GB VRAM. Follow the live readiness report for the path you choose.

An exported checkpoint and a lower loss are not proof of audible improvement. Compare a matched baseline and adapter take, then try a held-out prompt. Do not assume the native GGUF song engine loads the export.

Share the hypothesis, compatible engine/slots, settings and what you actually heard, including no useful change.

9 · Direct one video shot

This session is one shot, not a whole render queue.

Open Video in Full Studio. Use an engine whose setup, hardware and licence checks fit your situation. Write one subject, one action and one camera idea. Start with a short representative pilot.

Watch motion with sound, including the first/last seconds. Check geometry, object count, subject scale and continuity. If it fails, change one variable and keep the earlier take. A thumbnail cannot judge a clip.

Discussion: was the failure in the shot design, reference, seed or an unresolved engine limit? It is fine to say we do not know yet.

10 · H3 references and comparisons

H3 pilot session: read the current Models licence/territory notice first. Install H3 components plus Video references — MiniMax H3 ref2va.

Video → Advanced → MiniMax H3. Leave component files on auto. References → Add a picture…; name it with <Picture 1>. Use one subject/action, a short shot and saved seed. Verify the displayed steps before Render clip. Do not add the automatic Turbo adapter again as a custom LoRA.

Eight-step references prefer installed REF2V V1.1 with V1.0 fallback; four-step references use their separate adapter. One matched Singularity V2 pair on RTX 4070 Ti SUPER 16 GB measured 260.611 → 246.861 s (5.276% less), with MORE scene drift. Not a universal speed, quality or lip-sync result.

Watch the actual clip. https://github.com/Senzube4n/AIPLAY-Studio/blob/main/docs/H3_REF_TURBO.md

11 · Plan a music video

Now we connect the pieces in Production.

Attach the accepted song → check the beat grid → Creative interview → Script & direction → Story review → Characters & props / Backgrounds / Storyboards → Video clips.

The current UI needs an outside MCP assistant for the first whole production bible; after that its parts are editable in Studio. Ask the assistant to read mv_bible_spec, write the proposed bible and run free review checks before any render.

Use the shared directors.md template for the brief, exact names, approved scope and handoff. Share a scene plan, not a promise of a finished video.

12 · Cut, finish and export

A timeline project is not yet a delivered video file.

Choose your scene takes, Move to Studio, then edit the rhythm and joins. Preserve your cut before Rebuild the Studio timeline; rebuilding replaces its composed timeline with current picks.

Use titles, grades and transitions for polish. Where available, Render the finished video uses ffmpeg; Studio Export video captures the browser canvas in real time. The offline route combines clips with one audio item and does not reproduce every title, effect or multiple-audio mix. Check these or bake finishing into source media, then review the actual exported file.

Bring a short public-safe cut and timecoded feedback: which transition, sound or shot needs the smallest useful repair?

13 · Films, series and agent handoffs

For longer stories, grow the planning before the render queue.

Use one project per episode and a shared series bible. Carry the actual approved character/reference images into each project; matching prose alone does not preserve a face. Keep the ending continuity facts for the next episode.

Connect Codex or Claude through Agent → MCP server, verify with read-only capability/readiness calls, and tell the assistant to read directors.md. Keep private keys and paths out of shared config examples.

Final exercise: hand a two-scene plan to a fresh assistant. Can it recover the chosen references, current state, evidence and next useful action?
