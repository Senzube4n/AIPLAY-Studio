Native score planning and replay
Generate a score, edit it, then render. Saved semantic tokens can be rendered again without resampling the song. Exact lyrics and model/runtime hashes travel with the run; identical audio is not guaranteed.






Hybrid styles
Try concrete roles: “warm melodic synthpop verses, chopped glitch percussion in the chorus, stuttering fills before each drop, expressive vocals.” Compare several seeds and revise the score. Model adherence varies; extra descriptive text is an experiment.


Dataset and native training
Choose library recordings, review each song, then prepare. Preparation retries skip completed songs. Native training requires an NVIDIA GPU with at least 11 GB VRAM. Existing Studio training remains available.
